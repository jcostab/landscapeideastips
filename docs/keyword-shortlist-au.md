# AU Keyword Shortlist for Landscape Ideas Tips

**Research Date:** 13 September 2026  
**Source:** Ahrefs Keywords Explorer (country: Australia)  
**Volumes:** Average monthly AU searches

## Methodology

This shortlist was compiled using Ahrefs Keywords Explorer with Australia as the target country. Focus was placed on:

- **Informational and commercial-intent keywords** (over purely transactional)
- **Evergreen topics** with consistent year-round or seasonal search patterns
- **Quality over filler** — keywords with meaningful search volume and realistic ranking opportunities
- **Melbourne/Peninsula climate angles** where natural to the topic
- **Low to moderate keyword difficulty (KD)** to maximize ranking potential

We excluded branded terms, off-topic noise (e.g., phone privacy screen protectors, lymphatic drainage), and pure product/transactional queries (e.g., Bunnings-only shopping terms).
